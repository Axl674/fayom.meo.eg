2. Self-Check: Unintentional Intersections & Clearance Cuts

  • Shared Partition Wall (X: 125..140): Refrigerant capillary Pipe_P4 and evaporator outlet Pipe_P6 cross from the dispenser column into the freezer compartment. Circular clearance holes (Ø14 mm) are cut at Y=600 and Y=694 (Z=-213) to eliminate solid collision.
  • Condenser Fins vs. Serpentine Tube (Pipe_P2): The 11 mm copper tube passes through the 16 aluminum fins (X=175..325, Z=185) by design as an interference-fit heat exchanger assembly.
